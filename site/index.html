          Our story

          Same room, new fire

          After a decade of cooking at 75 Sheep Street, we’ve evolved into Boca Tapas Bar & Grill — the same open kitchen and the same obsession with doing things properly, pointed firmly at Spain.

          Our chefs cook tapas to order: garlic and chilli in hot olive oil, things charring over flame, bread going into the oven. Nothing sits under a lamp. Nothing arrives lukewarm. If you can smell chorizo the moment you open the door, that’s the point.

          “Feed people the way you’d feed friends who happened to bring their appetite.”
